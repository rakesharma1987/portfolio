Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

Thank you for using Expense Tracker. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles your information.

Information We Collect

Expense Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

We do not collect:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Financial account information
  • Any other personal or sensitive information

Expense Data

The app stores only the expense information that you enter, such as:

  • Expense title
  • Expense amount
  • Expense category
  • Date
  • Notes (if added by you)

This information is stored only on your device using local storage. It is never uploaded to any server.

No Internet Data Collection

Expense Tracker does not use any backend server or web service to collect, store, or process your data.

The application does not use any APIs to send your expense information anywhere.

No Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking services, or cloud storage.

Data Security

Since all information remains on your own device, you have complete control over your data. Removing the application or clearing its data may permanently delete your stored expense records.

Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or users of any age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
