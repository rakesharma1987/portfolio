Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

Thank you for using our applications. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications handle your information.

Information We Collect

Our applications are designed to work primarily using local storage on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information.

We do not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home or mailing address
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Financial information
  • Government-issued identification
  • Any other personal or sensitive information

Application Data

Any information you create or enter while using our applications is stored only on your device. This data is never uploaded, synchronized, or transmitted to our servers because our applications do not operate a backend service for user data.

The type of locally stored data depends on the purpose of each application and may include user-created content, preferences, notes, records, or other information entered by you.

Local Storage

Our applications use your device's local storage or local database solely to provide application functionality. All stored information remains on your device unless you choose to delete it.

No Internet Data Collection

Our applications do not send your personal or application data to any server owned or operated by us.

We do not use our own servers or APIs to collect, process, or store your information.

No Third-Party Tracking

Our applications do not use third-party analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, profiling, or cloud storage services to collect your personal information.

Permissions

Some applications may request device permissions (such as camera, storage, notifications, or similar permissions) only to enable features that you choose to use. These permissions are used solely on your device and are not used to collect or transmit your personal information.

Data Security

Because your data remains stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. Uninstalling an application or clearing its data may permanently remove all locally stored information.

Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children or users of any age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
