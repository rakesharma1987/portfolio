Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 16, 2026

Thank you for using our applications. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Some of our applications allow users to store business-related information locally on their device to provide application functionality.

Depending on the application, the information entered by users may include:

  • Customer Name
  • Customer Mobile Number
  • Vehicle Registration Number
  • Vehicle Details
  • Service History
  • Business Records
  • Notes entered by the user
  • Other information voluntarily entered by the user

This information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

The information you enter is used solely to provide the features of the application, such as maintaining customer records, vehicle information, inventory, service history, reports, or other business functions.

We do not access, analyze, or use this information for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

Application Data

All data entered into our applications is stored locally on your device using the device's local database or storage.

We do not upload, synchronize, process, or back up your data to our servers because our applications do not use cloud storage or backend services for user data.

Local Storage

Our applications use your device's local storage or local database solely to provide application functionality.

Customer information, vehicle details, inventory records, notes, and other application data remain stored only on your device unless you choose to export, share, or delete the information.

Internet Usage

Our applications do not transmit your customer information or business data to our servers.

If an application uses an internet connection, it is only for features such as application updates or other services that do not involve uploading your locally stored business data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with any third party.

Customer information stored within the application remains on your device and is accessible only through the application unless you intentionally export or share it yourself.

Third-Party Services

Our applications do not use third-party analytics, advertising, user profiling, or tracking services to collect your personal or customer information.

Permissions

Some applications may request permissions such as:

  • Camera
  • Storage
  • Notifications
  • Internet

These permissions are requested only to provide application features selected by you. They are not used to collect or transmit your personal information to our servers.

Data Security

Since all information is stored locally on your device, protecting your device is your responsibility.

If you uninstall the application or clear its storage, all locally stored information may be permanently deleted unless you have created your own backup.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Control Over Your Data

Because your data is stored only on your device, you have complete control over it. You may edit, delete, export, or remove your data at any time using the application's features or by uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
